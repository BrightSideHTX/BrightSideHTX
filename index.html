FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Find answers to some of the most common questions about BrightSide Detailing.

What areas do you serve?

BrightSide Detailing serves Alief and surrounding Houston communities within approximately 20 miles of our service area.

Is BrightSide a mobile detailing service?

Yes. We bring the detailing service directly to your home, workplace, or another eligible location.

What days are appointments available?

BrightSide currently offers detailing appointments on weekends. Available dates and times can be viewed through our online booking system.

How do I book a detail?

Start by visiting our booking page. Enter your service location to make sure it is within our service area, then continue to schedule your detail.

Do prices change depending on my vehicle?

Yes. Pricing varies based on vehicle size and may vary depending on the condition of the vehicle. Any additional charges will be confirmed before service.

What if my vehicle has heavy stains or pet hair?

Additional charges may apply for heavy staining, excessive pet hair, or unusually dirty vehicles. Final pricing will be confirmed before service.

Do I need to provide anything for the detail?

Details about service requirements and your appointment will be provided during the booking process.

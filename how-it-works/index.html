PROGRAM DETAILS

Good To Know.

ELIGIBILITY

Completed Paid Services

Rewards are issued only for referrals who complete and pay for a BrightSide detailing service.

CREDITS

Combine Your Rewards

Referral credits may be combined with other referral credits, but they cannot be exchanged for cash.

DISCOUNTS

Promotional Discounts

Referral credits cannot be combined with other promotional discounts.

VERIFICATION

Referral Confirmation

BrightSide may verify referrals before issuing a credit to make sure the program is being used correctly.

How It Works.

From checking your service area to getting your vehicle detailed, the process is simple. Here's what to expect when you book with BrightSide.

THE PROCESS

Three Steps. That's It.

We've kept the booking process straightforward so you can spend less time figuring things out and more time enjoying a clean vehicle.

STEP 01

Check Your Service Area

Enter your service address on our booking page to make sure your location is within BrightSide's service area.

  • Enter your address
  • Select your address from the suggestions
  • View your location on the map
  • Confirm you're within our service area

Serving Alief and surrounding Houston communities within approximately 20 miles of the Alief Neighborhood Center.

STEP 02

Choose Your Detail

Choose the service that fits what your vehicle needs. Pricing depends on the size of your vehicle.

  • Exterior Detail starting at $75
  • Interior Detail starting at $100
  • Full Detail starting at $150
  • Optional add-ons available

YOUR APPOINTMENT

We Come To You.

BrightSide is a mobile detailing service, meaning you don't have to drive to a shop and wait around for your vehicle to be finished.

01

Before We Arrive

Complete your booking and make sure your vehicle is accessible at the scheduled location.

02

During Your Detail

We'll work through the services included in your selected package and give your vehicle the attention it needs.

03

When We're Done

Your vehicle will be ready for you to inspect once the detail is complete.

BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

A Little Preparation Goes A Long Way.

A few simple things can help your appointment go smoothly and give us more time to focus on your vehicle.

ACCESS

Make Your Vehicle Accessible

Make sure the vehicle is accessible when we arrive. This helps us get started on time and move through the service efficiently.

PERSONAL ITEMS

Remove Important Belongings

Please remove valuable or important personal belongings from your vehicle before the appointment.

VEHICLE CONDITION

Let Us Know About Extra Needs

Heavy staining, excessive pet hair, or unusually dirty vehicles may require additional work. Any additional charge will be discussed before service begins.

PRICING

Know The Price Before You Book.

BrightSide uses vehicle size to determine starting prices. This lets you see the expected starting cost before scheduling your appointment.

01

Vehicle Size

Sedan, SUV, truck, and large SUV/van pricing is listed on the Packages page.

02

Add-Ons

Additional services such as pet hair removal and heavy stain removal are available when needed.

03

Final Confirmation

Additional charges related to vehicle condition will be discussed before service begins.
